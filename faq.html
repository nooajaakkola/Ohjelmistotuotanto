The product

How does the keyboard turn into a controller?

Hybrid CTRL transforms almost instantly from a 60% keyboard (340 mm x 160 mm) into a controller (170 mm x 160 mm). You can switch between the two as often as you like, even in the middle of a gaming session.

What kind of switches does it have?

Magnetic rapid trigger switches. They register keypresses quickly and precisely, and they are built to survive the constant switching between keyboard and controller mode.

How fast is it?

The polling rate is 8000 Hz, so your inputs reach the game with minimal delay.

How long does the battery last?

About 2 weeks of normal use without charging.

Is there an accessory for gaming on the go?

We are planning an optional display and an extra battery for mobile gaming. They are not available to order yet. Send us a message and we will let you know when they are.

Compatibility

Which devices does it work with?

PC and all major gaming consoles, including PS5, Xbox Series X and Nintendo Switch.

How do I connect it?

Hybrid CTRL supports Bluetooth 6.3 and also comes with a USB receiver, so you can pick whichever works best for your device.
